Research landscape

Five connected questions run through the work.

The topics have changed over time, but the underlying interest is consistent: how can we represent knowledge, make better technical decisions and build systems that remain understandable as they become more intelligent?

01

Responsible & Green AI

How can AI deliver useful outcomes while making energy, emissions, trust and human oversight visible?

02

Knowledge systems & software engineering

How can explicit knowledge help engineers design software faster, with clearer reasoning and stronger reuse?

03

Digital twins & cyber-physical systems

How can digital representations stay connected to real industrial behaviour, change and operational context?

04

Robotics & control synthesis

How can control solutions be generated or reconfigured from what a system knows about its resources and capabilities?

05

Healthcare & scientific computing

How can knowledge graphs, imaging methods and emerging computation support safer scientific and clinical reasoning?
