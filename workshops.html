01 · One-day applied learning program

Innovation in Academia

A faculty transformation lab for stronger patents, funded research and industry relevance.

Research novelty · Patent potential · Fundable partnerships
AudienceTeachers and academic leaders
Duration1 day · 9:30 to 17:00
FormatHands-on studio
InvestmentINR 3,500 per person

Why this matters now

Turn faculty expertise into a visible pipeline of novel research, defensible IP and fundable partnerships.

What participants gain

  • Identify consequential and underexplored problems.
  • Move beyond incremental publication towards defensible contribution.
  • Shape novel, non-obvious and patent-ready concepts.
  • Build credible pathways to industry and government funding.

What we will explore

  • Inventive thinking for research and institutional challenges
  • Genuine novelty versus routine extensions
  • Research storytelling, evidence, IP and stakeholder value

Who should attend

  • University and college teachers
  • Heads of department and deans
  • Research supervisors and lab leaders
  • Curriculum and faculty-development teams

Takeaway toolkit

  • Mapped portfolio of research problems
  • Novelty and non-obviousness canvas
  • TRIZ and reverse-brainstorming concepts
  • 30-day experiment, IP and engagement roadmap
View the one-day learning journey
  1. 09:30 to 10:15Beyond the next paper: recognise the iterative publication trap and define meaningful impact.
  2. 10:15 to 11:15Novel problem discovery: scan tensions, unmet needs and emerging opportunities.
  3. 11:30 to 12:45Storytelling for relevance: connect evidence, beneficiaries and urgency.
  4. 13:45 to 15:00Framework lab: TRIZ contradictions and structured idea generation.
  5. 15:15 to 16:15Reverse brainstorming and IP lens: test novelty and non-obviousness.
  6. 16:15 to 17:00Impact pathway: map experiments, patents, partners and funding routes.

Methods and included value

Novel problem landscapes, research storytelling, TRIZ, reverse brainstorming, publication-to-patent decision lens and industry collaboration canvas.

Workbook, innovation tool cards, IP lens, funding canvas and participation certificate are included. Institutional cohort pricing is available for 25 to 40 participants.

03 · One-day applied learning program

Connecting Technology to Emotions

Build campus capability in human-centred AI, responsible technology and experience innovation.

Human insight · Responsible AI · Interdisciplinary innovation
AudienceStudents, teachers and designers
Duration1 day · 9:30 to 17:00
FormatDesign and prototype lab
InvestmentFrom INR 2,000

Why this matters now

Position the institution at the intersection of human-centred AI, responsible technology and interdisciplinary innovation.

What participants gain

  • Reveal human-technology problems worth researching.
  • Move beyond feature iterations to meaningful experience innovation.
  • Shape non-obvious concepts with patent potential.
  • Present an ethical, partner-ready case for collaboration.

What we will explore

  • Emotional tensions as sources of technology problems
  • Stories and empathy evidence for responsible design
  • Usefulness, privacy, trust and inclusion

Who should attend

  • Engineering, design, management and psychology students
  • Teachers and researchers in HCI and AI
  • Product, UX and innovation teams in academic incubators

Takeaway toolkit

  • Emotion journey map grounded in evidence
  • Human-technology tensions and design principles
  • TRIZ-generated differentiated concepts
  • Prototype with trust, inclusion and ethics checks
View the one-day learning journey
  1. 09:30 to 10:15Technology is an emotional experience: trust, anxiety, agency and belonging as innovation signals.
  2. 10:15 to 11:15Find unmet needs: empathy interviews and bias-aware observation.
  3. 11:30 to 12:45Map the experience: personas, emotion journeys and moments that matter.
  4. 13:45 to 15:00Invent around tensions: use TRIZ for privacy, personalisation, control and care.
  5. 15:15 to 16:15Reverse brainstorm and IP lens: turn failure modes into differentiated concepts.
  6. 16:15 to 17:00Prototype with care: pitch value, emotional signals and ethical safeguards.

Methods and included value

Emotion-led problem discovery, empathy interviews, journey maps, inclusive design, affective computing foundations, TRIZ, IP lens and responsible testing.

Students: INR 2,000. Faculty and professionals: INR 3,000. Workbook, empathy, IP and ethics canvases, prototype materials and certificate are included.
