Facilitation approach

A structured path from insight to action.

The sequence is customised to the engagement, but the experience remains practical, collaborative and outcome-led.

01

Discover

Surface strategic tensions, unmet needs, stakeholder pain points and underexplored opportunities.

02

Frame

Translate observations into clear problem statements, decision questions and desired outcomes.

03

Invent

Use structured creativity, TRIZ principles, reverse brainstorming and cross-domain analogies.

04

Evaluate

Test value, feasibility, novelty, risk, ethics, trust, adoption and stakeholder relevance.

05

Communicate

Build an evidence-based narrative for leaders, partners, employers, funders or reviewers.

06

Act

Define experiments, owners, milestones, decision gates and a practical action roadmap.
