# Website analytics summary

The automated 30-day aggregate export will populate `summary.csv` and `summary.json` after the Cloudflare collector is deployed.

For quick review in GitHub, open `analytics/summary.csv`.

The public summary is intentionally limited to aggregate counts such as visitors, sessions, pages, countries, cities, referrers and engagement-event totals. Detailed pseudonymous visitor journeys remain in the protected analytics database/export and are not committed to this public repository.
